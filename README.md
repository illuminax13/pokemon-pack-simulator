# Rip City

Rip City (formerly Rippermon) is a browser booster-pack simulator for Pokémon TCG and Disney Lorcana. Pick a set, swipe the 3D pack carousel, tear open your pack and reveal the cards, then collect them in binders. Card data and images come from pokemontcg.io and TCGdex.

Live: https://ripcity.netlify.app

Moving from the old name: open https://rippermon.netlify.app once on the device that has your collection. It moves your saved collection over to Rip City automatically. Backups exported under the old name (`rippermon-backup-*.json`) still import normally.

You can also open `index.html` locally.
